# Hotel Panamá Canal image sources

## Login background

`hotel-panama-canal-login.webp` is an AI-enhanced aerial reference still of Hotel Panamá Canal in Espinar, Colón, beside Lake Gatún. It is an edited background image, not an unmodified original photograph.

Source: [Mall Colón Puerto Libre's collaborative Instagram reel with @hotelpanamacanal](https://www.instagram.com/mallcolon/reel/DdkitKRs8GO/), published September 21, 2026. The source was inspected and captured on October 2, 2026. The edit preserves the visible hotel, pool, lake and overall framing while improving clarity and removing the surrounding Instagram interface and promotional subtitles. Small reconstructed details should not be treated as a factual property inventory.
